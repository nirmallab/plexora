{# The figure canvas. A three-column editor: what the figure is made of on the left (Layers, Assets, Insert), the sheet in the middle, and what the selection is on the right (the inspector). Both columns are docked, collapsible, and take their width FROM the canvas rather than lying over it -- a card floating over the artwork is a card that has to be closed before anything under it can be judged, and judging the artwork is what this page is for. The inspector is contextual in its CONTENT, not in its presence. Nothing selected, it describes the page; one image, that image; several objects, how to line them up. It used to be the other way round -- a strip that came and went with the selection -- and every selection change slid the sheet sideways by three hundred pixels. The floating bar (figureContextBar.js) is still here, cut down to the few things done most often to the object under it; everything with a number in it is in the inspector. Both render from figureActions.js, as does the right-click menu. #fb_overlay_layer is the one part of this the canvas renderer never touches. FigureCanvas.render() replaces the whole of #fb_page_surface on every change, so anything that has to survive a render -- the context bar, an open text editor -- lives out here instead. #}
