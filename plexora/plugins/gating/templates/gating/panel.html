{# NO HEADING OF ITS OWN, and no close: this panel is the BODY of the card core builds for a loaded tool, and that card's header already carries the name, the key that opens it, an eye and an X. The CSV pair used to be lifted into that header as two unlabelled arrows whose only account of themselves was a tooltip -- the same mistake the base image's channel-rename button made, and fixed the same way. {# THE WHOLE GATE ON ONE LINE: `0.42 ---o=====o--- 9.10 *`, which is the image channel's contrast window exactly (viewerSidebar's createChannelSlot). The two thresholds had a row of their own above the track, and Auto Threshold a full-width button below it -- three lines for one control, in a 300px sidebar, when the numbers are three to six characters each. The numbers are the slider's own `.plx-number` boxes, inline at the two ends of the track they name: drawn as plain text until they are clicked (as every slider's numbers are, in main.css), still typeable, which is what a gate copied off a paper or from another sample has always wanted. The button at the end is Auto, and afterwards the way back from it. #}
{# WHERE THE THREE INPUTS DISAGREE, if they do. A table, a mask and an image come from three steps of a pipeline and nothing in any of them says they belong together, so pairing a table with the wrong image, or a mask exported at another resolution, produces a panel that works and is answering about a different sample. Directly under the distribution, because the distribution is the thing being doubted: this is the marker's own numbers, and this is what may be wrong with the frame they are in. Quiet by construction -- muted text on the soft amber DESIGN.md keeps for a non-blocking warning -- because every one of these states is one a real project is allowed to be in.
