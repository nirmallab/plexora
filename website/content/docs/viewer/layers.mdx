The Layers section, below Image Channels in the sidebar, lists every layer
this sample draws — the reference image, and anything registered alongside
it (a second slide aligned to the first, a segmentation mask, additional
channels) — as one card each.

## What every card has

Whatever the layer, its card carries the same controls:

- a grip on the left to drag it, for reordering;
- a chevron to collapse or expand the card's own controls;
- the layer's name;
- an eye to show or hide it;
- a padlock to pin it in place, so a drag cannot move it by accident;
- an `×` to remove it — except on the reference image, which has none: the
  way to remove that one is to remove the sample itself.

The reference image's card also carries a colour dot for the ground it is
drawn on, and a `···` menu for copying or pasting channel names and rendering
settings between images. See [Adjust the display](/docs/viewer/display-controls)
for what the ground colour does.

## Show or hide a layer

Click the eye on a card to toggle whether that layer is drawn. Hiding a layer
is instant and keeps everything about it — which channels are on, their
colours, its position in the stack — so showing it again looks exactly as it
did.

## Opacity

Each card has an opacity slider running from 0 to 100%. On the reference
image and on any layer with its own channels, the value is shown compactly as
a percentage next to the card's other actions; click it to reveal the track
behind it. Dragging updates the image immediately.

## Reorder by drag

Grab a card by its grip and drag it up or down the list to change which
layer draws over which. The topmost card in the list is the topmost layer on
the image. A locked layer (padlock closed) cannot be dragged until you
unlock it.

Images composite as one group of rasters; a segmentation mask's own points
and any shapes a plugin draws sit on a separate surface above them. A card
can only be dragged past another card on the same surface, so you can
reorder among your registered images freely, and separately reorder among
point or shape layers, but not mix the two.

## Alignment

Every registered layer but the reference image shows a short note at the
foot of its card, saying what its registration amounts to:

- **Registered**, with a summary of the move, rotation, scale or mirror
  applied — this layer has a real, known alignment to the reference image;
- **Aligned by assumption** — this layer was registered with no transform at
  all, so Plexora is drawing it exactly where the reference image is with
  nothing to say that lines up;
- **Cannot be drawn: shear** (or another unsupported transform) — this
  layer's registration includes something Plexora's viewer cannot express,
  so it is flagged rather than drawn subtly wrong.

## Add a layer

Click the `+` beside the Layers heading to add another layer to this sample —
a second image, a mask, or a table — through the same dialog used to import a
new sample, scoped to add to this one instead. This is the progressive half
of importing: bring in the reference image first, and attach what else you
have as you get it.

## Remove a layer

Click the `×` on a layer's card to take it out of the sample. Plexora asks
for confirmation first, and the underlying file is left exactly where it is
on disk — removing a layer only stops Plexora from drawing it.
