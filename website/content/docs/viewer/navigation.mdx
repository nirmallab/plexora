The viewer opens with the whole image fit to the window. From there you can pan,
zoom to any level of detail, jump back to the starting view, and — on a
multiplexed or spatial image — open a small overview map to see where you are
on the slide.

## Pan

Click and drag anywhere on the image to pan. The cursor changes to an open
hand at rest and a closed hand while you drag, so you always know whether a
press will move the view.

## Zoom

Scroll to zoom in and out under the pointer. The image also has a zoom-in and
zoom-out button in the toolbar at the bottom right of the canvas, alongside
Home and the full-screen button.

With the canvas focused (click it once), the arrow keys pan, `+` and `-` zoom,
and `0` returns to the home view — OpenSeadragon's own bindings underneath the
viewer. `W`, `A`, `S` and `D` pan the same way once the canvas has focus.

Zooming out stops well before the slide shrinks to a speck, and zooming in
keeps going past the image's native resolution — useful for inspecting
individual cells closely, particularly with [HD tile quality](/docs/viewer/hd-mode)
turned on.

## Home

The Home button in the toolbar fits the whole image back into the window.
Rotating or flipping the view (see [Rotate or flip the view](/docs/viewer/rotation-and-flip))
does not change what Home fits to — it always fits the image as currently
turned.

## The overview map

A multiplexed or spatial image has a small circular button in the bottom-left
corner of the canvas. Click it to open an overview of the whole tissue, drawn
in the same channels, colours and contrast you are currently viewing, with a
rectangle marking what is on screen.

- Drag the rectangle to pan.
- Click anywhere else on the map to recentre on that point.
- Scroll over the map to zoom.

Click the button again (it now shows a small cross) to close the map. The
overview is not built until you first open it, so it costs nothing on a
project you never use it on. A flat RGB image (for example, an image opened
from the quick-look home page with no channels) has no overview map.


## Full screen

The full-screen button in the toolbar hides the browser's own chrome and the
Plexora navbar, leaving only the sidebar and the image. Press it again, or
press Escape, to return. In the desktop app this toggles the app window's own
full-screen mode instead of the browser's.
