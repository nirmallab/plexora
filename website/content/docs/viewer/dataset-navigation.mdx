Looking at one sample in a dataset almost always means looking at the next
one. The Previous / Next control, in the top-right corner of the canvas,
moves through a dataset's samples without sending you back to the Samples
page in between.

## Previous and Next

The control is muted until the pointer is near it, so it stays out of the
way of the image. Each arrow is disabled at whichever end of the dataset you
are at, and its tooltip names the neighbour it would open — for example,
"Next sample: sample_04 (N)".

Samples are walked in the order they were added to the dataset, not the order
the Samples page happens to sort them in (which changes with what you last
opened) — so Next twice never lands you back where you started.

Click the counter in the middle (for example, "3 / 12") to drop a grid of
every sample's thumbnail underneath, and click any one to jump straight to
it.

## What carries across

Moving to another sample is a full page load — the arrangement is carried
across it, not kept on an unchanging page. The rule is that an **arrangement**
travels and a **measurement** does not: things you chose, and would choose
again, move with you; readings taken off this particular image's pixels do
not.

Carried:

- which channels are on, and each one's colour;
- whether [HD tile quality](/docs/viewer/hd-mode) is on;
- the Cells mode, point size and opacity;
- which layers are visible, and their opacity;
- which tools are open, how their cards are arranged, and — for tools that
  support it — what each one was doing (for example, which column
  [Cell Explorer](/docs/plugins/cell-explorer) was colouring by);
- the [Transcripts](/docs/viewer/transcript-rendering) section's own state,
  such as which genes are selected.

Not carried — the next sample keeps its own saved values, or starts fresh:

- each channel's contrast window;
- gate thresholds in [Thresholding](/docs/plugins/thresholding);
- the current viewport (pan position and zoom level).

If something could not be carried across — a column that does not exist on
the next sample, for instance — a notice names what was skipped. If nothing
in common could be found at all, the notice says the sample opened fresh
rather than leaving you to wonder whether something went wrong.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **B** or Page Up for the previous sample, **N** or Page Down for the
next, from anywhere the image itself has focus. These are disabled while a
routed page like Settings is open over the viewer.
