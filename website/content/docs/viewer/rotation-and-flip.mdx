---
title: "Rotate or flip the view"
description: "Turn the whole view to a right angle or any angle, and mirror it, without losing anything drawn over the image."
aliases: ["rotate", "flip", "mirror", "orientation"]
---

**Rotate & Flip** turns and mirrors the whole view — every channel, the mask,
any overlay and any registered layer together — as one tool, because which
way round the image is shown is one question. Open it from the View menu, or
from the Tools menu on a sample that has one.

## Rotate

Click one of the four right-angle buttons (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°) for a quick
turn, or drag the slider underneath for any angle in between. The number
beside the slider is also editable directly: click it and type a value in
degrees.

A right-angle click animates the turn; dragging the slider follows your hand
directly.

## Flip

Two buttons beside the rotation control mirror the view on the screen's own
axes — horizontally (left and right, as you currently see them) or vertically
(top and bottom) — independent of each other and of the current rotation.
Each keeps its own state: pressing one never changes the other, or the angle.

With a mirror on, a larger rotation angle turns the picture the other way on
screen, because the mirror has reversed which way is which.

## Reset

The reset button beside the slider returns the view to 0° with no flips.
It is disabled while the view is already at 0°.

## What this changes, and what it saves

Turning or flipping the view affects everything drawn on the image — every
channel, the segmentation mask, cell overlays, and any registered layer —
and any text drawn over the image (the sample's name, the scale bar) mirrors
along with it. [Home](/docs/viewer/navigation) fits the image as currently
turned, not its original orientation.

The orientation is saved with the image, so it opens the same way next time.
Closing the Rotate & Flip card leaves the view exactly as it is — there is
nothing to confirm or discard.
