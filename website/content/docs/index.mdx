Plexora is a viewer and analysis tool for large multiplexed microscopy images and spatial transcriptomics data, opened on the machine where the data already is — a laptop, a lab workstation, a notebook, or an HPC cluster. These guides take you from installing it to importing your first sample, working from Python, and extending it with plugins.
