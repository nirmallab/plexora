Plexora has a feedback menu at the right of the navigation bar, the speech-bubble icon beside the connections globe. It works the same in the desktop app, in your browser and on the website.

## Send a report

1. Click the feedback icon and choose **Report a Bug** or **Request a Feature**. **Help › Report a Bug** and **Help › Request a Feature** open the same form.
2. If you are signed in to Plexora, the form shows the email on your BioCognia account, and replies go there. Otherwise, enter the email address you want us to reply to.
3. Describe what happened and what you expected, or what you would like Plexora to do. The steps that led to a problem help most.
4. Optionally, attach up to three files: screenshots (PNG, JPEG or WebP) or `.txt` and `.log` files, 5 MB each. You can also paste a screenshot or drop files onto the form.
5. Click **Send Report** or **Send Request**.

If the report cannot be sent, the form says why and keeps everything you entered, so you can send it again.

<Callout type="warn">
Log and text files can contain file paths and dataset names. Look through a log before you attach it.
</Callout>

## What is sent with it

Plexora sends a few diagnostics with every report, so we can reproduce what you saw. Open **See what is sent** at the bottom of the form to read the exact list before you send. It contains:

- the Plexora version and build, and whether it runs as the desktop app, in a browser or on the website
- your operating system, browser, screen size, language and time zone
- the kind of page you were on (for example the viewer or Settings) and the tool that was open
- the last few errors Plexora caught in this window, with file paths, web addresses and email addresses removed

It never contains your files, your images or tables, project or dataset names, or your licence.

## When it is fixed

We read every report. When a bug you reported is fixed, or a feature you asked for is ready, we email you once to say it is coming in the next Plexora release. Choose **Help › Check for Updates** to install it when it is out. On the website, the new version loads the next time you open it.
