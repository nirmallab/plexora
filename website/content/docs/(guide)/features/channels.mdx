The sidebar's **Image** card holds one row per channel shown: a marker, its colour and its contrast. Up to 15 rows can be on at once. The contrast bar above the rows sets the window of the selected row.

<Media src="/media/features/channels-panel.webp" alt="The Image card in the sidebar: the contrast bar reading Contrast · CD45 with a range slider, and channel rows each with an eye, a colour swatch and a marker name, one row marked Selected" caption="The Image card. The selected row's contrast is set on the bar above the rows." />

## How to use it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Pick a marker for a row

Click a row's name and type to search the image's channels. **+** adds a row; **×** on a row removes it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Turn rows on and off

Click a row's eye. Press on one eye and drag down the column to switch several at once. The eye on the contrast bar shows or hides every row.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose a colour

Click a row's colour swatch and pick a colour.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set the contrast

Click a row to select it, then drag the two ends of the bar, or type a number at either end. **Histogram** shows the channel's distribution under the same range. **Clear** goes back to the full range; the row's wand (**Auto contrast**) fits a window for you, and pressed again restores the previous one.
</Step>
<Step>
### Step through markers

**K** and **J** replace the selected row's marker with the next or previous channel, so you can look through a panel one marker at a time. **Esc** puts back what was there.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Media src="/media/features/channels-histogram.webp" alt="The Histogram popover under the contrast bar, showing a channel's intensity distribution with the contrast window marked" caption="Histogram: the distribution behind the selected channel." />

## Tips

- Drag a row to reorder it.
- Colours, windows and the rows themselves are saved with the sample, so it opens as you left it.
- The **•••** menu on the Image card copies channel names or rendering settings to another sample, and turns on **Log brightness**, which shows dim cells and a faint background without losing bright detail.
- **Upload channel names** reads names from a CSV or a spreadsheet when the file has none.
