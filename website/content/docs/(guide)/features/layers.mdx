**Layers** in the sidebar lists everything the sample draws, one card each: the image itself, and anything added to it, such as a second image or a mask. The top card is drawn on top.

<Media src="/media/features/layers-panel.webp" alt="The Layers section with two layer cards, each with a grip, a chevron, a name, an eye and a padlock" caption="Every card has a grip, a chevron, an eye, a padlock and, except on the image itself, an ×." />

## How to use it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Show or hide a layer

Click the card's eye. Hiding a layer keeps its settings.
</Step>
<Step>
### Fade it

Click the opacity value on the card and drag the slider.
</Step>
<Step>
### Reorder

Drag a card by its grip. Close a card's padlock to pin it so a drag cannot move it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add a layer

Click **+** beside the Layers heading (**Add a layer to this sample**). It opens the import dialog for this sample: add another image, a mask or a table.
</Step>
<Step>
### Remove a layer

Click **×** and confirm. Plexora stops drawing it; the file stays where it is.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- Points a plugin draws stay above images whatever the order: a card can only be dragged among cards of the same kind.
- An added image's card says how it lines up with the first one: **Registered**, **Aligned by assumption** (no transform was given), or **Cannot be drawn** when its transform is one the viewer cannot show.
