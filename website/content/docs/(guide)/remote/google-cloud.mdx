If your images are in a Google Cloud Storage bucket, Plexora can put them next to a computer: it starts a Compute Engine VM in your Google Cloud account (or uses one you already run), mounts the bucket on it, and connects. Everything is created in your account and billed to it.

<Media src="/media/remote/gcloud-form.webp" alt="The Google Cloud form: project, bucket, the VM, machine type and VM location" />

## Before you start

You need the Google Cloud CLI on your computer (the form offers to install it), a project with billing enabled and the Compute Engine API turned on, and permission to create VMs and firewall rules plus the "IAP-secured Tunnel User" role.

## How to use it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose Google Cloud

Open **Add a server…** (see [Add a server](/docs/remote/add-a-server)) and choose **Google Cloud (GCP)**. Press **Sign in with Google**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the project and the bucket

Pick the **Google Cloud project** the VM is created and billed in, and the **Cloud Storage bucket** with your images. The bucket becomes Plexora's data directory on the VM. A public bucket can be typed in even if it is not in your project.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the VM

Under **The VM**, **Create a new VM** lets Plexora make, start, stop and delete one for you. **Use an existing VM** connects to a machine you already run and never changes or deletes it. For a new VM, pick a **Machine type** and a **VM location**; **Use bucket region** keeps the computer next to the data.
</Step>
<Step>
### Decide what happens when you finish

**Provisioning**: **Spot (preemptible)** is much cheaper, but Google may reclaim the machine; **Standard** is full price and is never taken away. **When Plexora exits**: **Leave VM running**, **Stop VM** (the default; only the disk keeps billing) or **Delete VM**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Connect

Press **Save and connect**. The dialog shows **Starting the Compute Engine VM**, **Mounting your Cloud Storage bucket** and the rest of the connection. Then open the bucket's images from the **Local** chip like any other server.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- Nothing on the internet can reach the VM. Plexora goes in through Google's IAP tunnel and blocks every other inbound connection.
- A VM left with nobody connected switches itself off after **Idle shutdown time (minutes)** (30 by default, under **Advanced**).
- The saved server's card in **Settings › Remote servers** has **Start VM**, **Stop VM** and **Delete VM…**. Deleting a VM never deletes your bucket; Plexora cannot delete storage.
