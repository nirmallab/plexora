Large images usually live where they were made: a cluster's scratch space, a lab server, a university file server, a cloud bucket. Plexora leaves them there. It reads each file where it is, a piece at a time, and shows it on your computer next to your own projects.

## What stays where

| On your computer | On the remote machine |
| --- | --- |
| Projects, datasets and their settings | The image, mask and table files |
| Regions, gates and quality-control results | A data node or Plexora, while you are connected (not for SFTP) |
| Figures and exports | Nothing that Plexora writes, unless you ask for it |

Plexora connects with your system `ssh`, so your keys, your `~/.ssh/config`, jump hosts and Duo prompts work as they do in a terminal. Nothing is opened to the network: every connection is an SSH tunnel.

## Tips

- Tiled, pyramidal images (pyramidal OME-TIFF, OME-Zarr) read best over a network, because each view fetches only the tiles on screen.
- A disconnect does not lose anything you made: your projects never left your computer. Reconnect and the layers come back.
