On a SLURM cluster, Plexora logs in to the login node, asks the scheduler for a job, waits in the queue, and connects to whichever compute node the job lands on. Reading and computing happen on that node, next to the data.

<Media src="/media/remote/slurm-form.webp" alt="The A Slurm cluster form: name, username, address, walltime, CPU cores and memory" />

## How to use it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose A Slurm cluster

Open **Add a server…** (see [Add a server](/docs/remote/add-a-server)) and choose **A Slurm cluster**, or your site under **Additional presets** if it is listed.
</Step>
<Step>
### Describe the job

Give **Address** (the login node) and **Your username on that machine**, then the job: **How long to keep it (walltime)**, **CPU cores** and **Memory** (4:00:00, 16 and 128G to start with). Anything else your site needs, such as `-p interactive`, goes in **Advanced › Other job options**; it is passed to `srun` as written, with the walltime, cores and memory added to it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Connect and wait for the job

Press **Save and connect**. After **Signing in**, the dialog shows **Waiting for the scheduler** while the job is queued. **Continue in background** lets you keep working; the dialog comes back if the cluster asks for a password or a Duo push.
</Step>
<Step>
### Work, then let it go

When the job starts, the server shows as **Connected** and its files open like local ones. **Disconnect** in **Settings › Remote servers** closes the connection.
</Step>
</Steps>

## From the command line

Everything above can also be done from a terminal; you do not need both. This one command queues the job and runs the whole of Plexora on the compute node, shown in your browser:

```bash
plexora connect me@login.cluster.edu --srun "-p interactive -t 4:00:00 -c 16 --mem 128G"
```

Add `--save NAME` to keep it as a saved server, and `--bind-node` where the site refuses SSH into compute nodes. See [Connect from a terminal](/docs/remote/ssh-connect).

## Tips

- Many clusters have no default partition. If the job is refused with "please specify partition with -p", add `-p <name>` to **Other job options**; `sinfo -s` on the login node lists the partitions.
- If your cluster refuses SSH into compute nodes, edit the saved server and turn on **Advanced › Forward from the login node**.
- The walltime is a hard limit: when it runs out the job ends and the connection drops. Reconnecting starts a new job; your projects are untouched.
