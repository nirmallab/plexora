ROI draws regions on the image (polygons, freehand shapes, rectangles, or outlines found by magic select) and keeps them with the sample. With a cell table, it can also label every cell with the region it falls in.

<Media src="/media/plugins/roi.webp" alt="The ROI card with three categories of regions listed, and the same regions drawn in their colours on the tissue" caption="Regions in three categories, drawn on the image and listed in the card." />

**Open it:** **Tools › ROI**. It works on any project; a region needs only the image.

## How to use it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Pick a category

Every region belongs to a category, such as Tumour or Stroma. A new project starts with one called **Undefined**. Add your own by typing a name in **Add new category** and pressing **Enter**, then click a category to draw into it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose a tool

While the ROI card is open, its tools sit in a bar at the top of the image: **Select** (**V**), **Polygon** (**P**), **Freehand** (**F**), **Rectangle** (**R**) and **Magic select** (**E**). None is chosen when the card opens, so dragging the image pans it until you pick one.
</Step>
<Step>
### Draw

A polygon is a click per point, finished with **Enter** or a double-click. Freehand and rectangle are a press and drag. Hold **Space** to pan while a drawing tool is in hand.
</Step>
<Step>
### Edit

With **Select**, click a region to select it, drag to move it, or drag a handle to reshape it. **F2** renames, **⌫** deletes, **⌘Z** undoes. A region's **⋯** menu renames, locks, hides, moves or deletes it.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Magic select

<Media src="/media/plugins/roi-magic-select.webp" alt="Magic select's bar on the image with Add, Remove, Box and Scribble, and a fold in the tissue outlined" />

Click something on the image (a fold, debris, a tumour nest) and Plexora outlines it. Picking magic select opens its options beside the wand in the tools bar; they set how you point at it, and clicking the wand again puts them away:

- **Add**: click to outline; click again to grow the outline.
- **Remove**: click an area to take it out (or **Shift**-click in Add).
- **Box**: drag round a large object.
- **Scribble**: draw over a long or patchy object, or a loop round it.

**Esc** finishes the outline. The outline follows the channels and contrast on screen. The first use downloads the files magic select needs; after that it works offline.

## Tips

- The eye on a row hides that region or category on the image; hidden regions are still saved and mapped.
- A locked region cannot be moved or reshaped.
- Regions belong to the image they were drawn on. Export them first if you want them on another image.

## What it saves

Regions are saved with the sample as you draw, in Plexora's own storage. The buttons at the foot of the card take them elsewhere:

- **Import** reads regions from a GeoJSON file; **Export** downloads them as GeoJSON.
- **Save** writes them into the project's own AnnData or SpatialData file (shown only for those projects).
- **Map to cells** (the grid button, shown when there is a cell table) writes two columns onto the cell table: the category and the region name of each cell, `rois_category` and `rois_name` by default. A cell in overlapping regions gets both values joined with `_`; cells outside every region are left blank. It shows what it will write and asks before replacing existing columns.
