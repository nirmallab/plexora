Figure Builder captures views of the image, with whatever another tool is drawing over it, and lays them out on a page with scale bars, labels, shapes and arrows. A figure can hold panels from several samples.

<Media src="/media/plugins/figure-dock.webp" alt="The Figure Builder capture dock over the image, with three captures in its strip and the Figure Canvas button" caption="The capture dock: every view you capture lands in its strip." />

**Open it:** **Tools › Figure Builder**, or press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux), to capture views. Your figures are under **File › Open Figures…**, or the **Figures** tab of the Samples page, with or without a sample open.

## How to use it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Capture a view

Press the dock's orb, or **C**, to turn on capture mode, then drag a rectangle over the region you want. The zoom, channels, contrast and any overlay are captured with it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick the captures

Each capture lands in the dock's strip. Click a thumbnail to go back to that view; tick it to use it in a figure.
</Step>
<Step>
### Open the canvas

Press **Figure Canvas** and choose an existing figure or a new one. The viewer gives way to the Figure Canvas, with your captures as panels.
</Step>
<Step>
### Arrange the page

Move, resize and re-crop panels. Add scale bars, text, shapes, lines and arrows. Everything is placed in millimetres, so the page prints at its true size.
</Step>
<Step>
### Export

Press **Export**, choose **PDF**, **TIFF** or **PNG** and a resolution of 150, 300, 600 or 1200 DPI.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Media src="/media/plugins/figure-canvas.webp" alt="The Figure Canvas with two image panels side by side, each with a scale bar and a label" />

## Tips

- A panel is re-rendered from the original image at export, at the resolution you ask for, not copied from the screen. Keep the source images where they are; moving them breaks the link.
- A scale bar reads the image's recorded pixel size. Without one, type a pixel size, or the bar is measured in pixels.
- Views you saved in the viewer with **M** are under **Bookmarks** on the canvas; **Add to figure** puts one straight on the page.
- Text uses Helvetica, Times or Courier, so every export format renders it the same way.

## What it saves

A figure saves itself as you edit (the canvas corner shows **Saving…**, then **Saved**). It stores the layout, crops, text and shapes, never a copy of the pixels. Captures wait in their sample's strip until you use them.

<Media src="/media/plugins/figure-export.webp" alt="The export dialog with format, resolution and the provenance page option" />

Export formats:

- **PDF**: text and legends stay editable.
- **TIFF**: flattened, for submission.
- **PNG**: flattened, for sharing; the only format that keeps transparency.

**Append a provenance page** adds a page recording what went into the figure. A single panel is limited to 16,000 pixels on its longest side; above that, export stops with a message instead of rendering.
