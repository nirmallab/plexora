Cell Explorer colours every cell by one column of the cell table (a phenotype call, a cluster label, a score, an area) and draws that colour over the tissue, so you can see where each population sits.

<Media src="/media/plugins/cell-explorer.webp" alt="Cells filled with colours by phenotype, and the Cell Explorer legend listing each phenotype with its colour and cell count" caption="Cells coloured by a phenotype column, with the legend." />

**Open it:** **Tools › Cell Explorer**, or press **⌘E** (Ctrl+E on Windows and Linux). It needs a cell table with a cell id, and either a segmentation mask or X/Y coordinates to draw the cells.

## How to use it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose a column

Click the field at the top of the card and pick a column; its dropdown searches every column on the table. **X** and **Z** (or **Next column** and **Previous column** at the foot of the card) step through the columns in the dropdown's order.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read a categorical column

A legend lists every category with its colour and cell count, under a line saying how many categories and cells the column has. Click a row to show or hide it, click its swatch to change its colour. **Show all** and **Hide all** switch the whole legend. The magnifying glass opens a filter that narrows the list without hiding anything on the image; **Esc** closes it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read a numeric column

A colour bar replaces the legend. Drag its ends to set the range, pick another palette, or press **Auto** to go back to the column's own range.
</Step>
<Step>
### Look inside a region

Press **Overlay ROI** at the foot of the card to open [ROI](/docs/plugins/roi) beside it, then rest the pointer on a region to see its cells broken down by the column on screen.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Media src="/media/plugins/cell-explorer-numeric.webp" alt="Cells coloured along a gradient by a numeric column, with the colour bar in the Cell Explorer card" />

## Tips

- A numeric column with only a few whole values (a cluster number, say) shows **Treat as**: choose **Categories** or **Numbers**.
- Cells are drawn **Filled** by default here. Lower **Opacity** in the **Cells** row at the bottom of the sidebar to see the tissue through them.
- To colour by region, run **Map to cells** in [ROI](/docs/plugins/roi) first, then pick its column here.

## What it saves

The column, each category's colour and visibility, and a numeric column's range are saved with the project, so the same view comes back next time. Cell Explorer only reads the cell table; it never writes to it.
