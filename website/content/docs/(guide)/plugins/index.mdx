Every tool in Plexora is a plugin: it opens as a card in the sidebar, next to the image, and draws on the image. Five come with Plexora.

<Cards>
<Card title="Quality Control" href="/docs/plugins/qc" description="Mark folds, blur and misregistered cycles, and flag the cells they affect." />

<Card title="ROI" href="/docs/plugins/roi" description="Draw regions, sort them into categories, and label cells by region." />
<Card title="Cell Explorer" href="/docs/plugins/cell-explorer" description="Colour every cell by a phenotype, cluster or numeric column." />
<Card title="Figure Builder" href="/docs/plugins/figure-builder" description="Capture views and lay them out as a figure for export." />

## How to use it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open a tool

With a sample open, choose it from the **Tools** menu, or press its shortcut, printed beside it in the menu.
</Step>
<Step>
### Use the card

The tool's card header has a grip, a chevron that folds the card, its name and shortcut, a **?** that explains the tool, an eye and a **×**. The eye hides what the tool draws on the image; **×** removes the tool.
</Step>
<Step>
### Open several

You can load several tools at once. Clicking a card's name opens it and folds the one that was open; each card keeps its own colours and opacity on the image.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Media src="/media/plugins/card-anatomy.webp" alt="The header of a tool card: grip, chevron, name and shortcut, help, eye and close" />

## Tips

- Drag a card by its grip to restack the tools: the order of the cards is the order their drawings are stacked on the image.
- A tool asks for what it needs (a cell table, which column is the cell id) the first time it needs it, and never again for the same project.
