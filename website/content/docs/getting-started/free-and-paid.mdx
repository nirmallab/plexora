Plexora has two plans. **Free** is everything Plexora does without a licence, for as long as you like. **Paid** adds Plexora's AI. A 30-day trial gives you everything in Paid with no card.

## Free

Every image, table and modality, every plugin's manual tools, remote and HPC viewing, notebooks and export. Free needs no account, no sign-up and no network, and it shows no nag. Nothing ever stops working: when a Paid licence or a trial ends, Plexora goes back to Free, and everything you made stays yours.

## Paid

- **Plexora AI.** AI agents that act on your data: guided gating sessions, AI quality control, and an assistant inside Plexora that answers questions about your projects. AI runs on your organisation's AI credits.
- **External MCP access.** Plexora's Paid features from an outside coding agent such as Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, over the Model Context Protocol, with that agent's own model.

Paid is a yearly subscription per seat, and each seat comes with 1,000 AI credits a year. Buy more credits whenever you need them.

Accounts, licences, seats and AI credits live on **BioCognia**, the platform Plexora shares with the lab's other tools, at [account.biocognia.com](https://account.biocognia.com). Plexora itself never sees a password, an email address or a card number.

## Start a free trial

A 30-day trial of everything in Paid, with 100 AI credits. No card. There is one trial per email address.

The quickest way is from the computer you want to use:

```bash
plexora license activate --trial
```

Approve the code in your browser and the trial starts on that computer. You can also:

- **Start on the web** at [account.biocognia.com](https://account.biocognia.com/portal/trial?product=plexora). Sign in with your email address. A one-time sign-in link arrives (there is no password), and your first sign-in creates your account. Choose **Start the trial**, then run `plexora license activate` on your computer.
- **Start in the app.** Choose any Paid feature, and pick **Start a Trial…** in the box that opens.

`plexora license status` shows the trial and when it ends. A trial has no grace period and cannot be used for an offline licence file.
