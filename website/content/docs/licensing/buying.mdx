Plexora Pro is a licence per seat for a year. A seat is one person, on up to two computers, with 1,000 AI credits a year. There is one price per seat, whatever the number of seats: the total is that price times the seats. Academic seats cost less than commercial ones. The [pricing page](/pricing) shows both prices and the total for any number of seats.

**A licence is one payment for one year.** Nothing renews by itself: you are emailed before it ends, and you renew by buying again.

Plexora Pro is bought at [biocognia.com](https://biocognia.com), BioCognia's own site, where every BioCognia product is sold. The pricing page's **Buy** takes you there with your choice filled in.

Pro adds Plexora AI, Plexora 3D, spatial transcriptomics and [Video Studio](/docs/plugins/video-studio): see [Free and Pro](/docs/getting-started/free-and-pro#pro). External MCP access is off by default and is not part of Pro; an administrator can turn it on for your licence.

## Academic or commercial

Academic covers students, postdocs, faculty and staff at universities, hospitals, government labs and other non-profit research organisations. Work funded by industry, done inside a company, or delivered as a service for a for-profit customer needs a commercial seat.

An academic price needs an email address at a recognised academic institution: the checkout checks the address you give when you continue. If yours is not recognised, you can ask for it to be reviewed.

## Buy

<Steps>
<Step>

On the [pricing page](/pricing), choose **Academic** or **Commercial** and the number of seats, then **Buy**. Both carry over into checkout.

</Step>
<Step>

You arrive at the checkout at [biocognia.com](https://biocognia.com/buy?offer=plexora), with Plexora Pro, the seats and the licence type filled in. You do not need an account: give your name, the email the licence keys go to, and who the invoice is addressed to (you, or your institution with its address, tax ID and purchase order). While sales are by invitation, Buy opens a short request for access instead, and the checkout link comes by email once you are approved.

</Step>
<Step>

**Continue to payment** opens Paddle's card form. Paddle, the merchant of record, takes the payment and handles VAT, GST and US sales tax. Your card never reaches BioCognia, and Plexora never sees any payment data.

</Step>
<Step>

The licence is made within a minute of the payment, with an account for that email if it has none. Sign in at [account.biocognia.com](https://account.biocognia.com) with that email to manage it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What arrives when you pay

A seat you buy for yourself is yours at once, and its **licence key** comes by email:

```
Your Plexora seat is ready (Pro, until 2027-10-21).

Your licence key:

    BIOC-7F3K-9Q2M-XW4T-7KQ2
```

Paste it in **Settings › License › Licence key**, or run `plexora license activate BIOC-…`. The same key works on each of your computers, up to the seat's limit (two, plus any you add), and keeps working when the licence renews.

## More computers for yourself

A seat covers two devices. To use Plexora on more, add devices to your own seat in pairs, each pair costing the same as one licence (there is no single device): at checkout when you buy for one person, or later under **Billing › Add capacity** at account.biocognia.com, priced for what is left of your licence's year. You keep one licence, one email and an individual account; added devices never add a person or make a team. Devices added for Plexora are Plexora's: a SCIMAP Pro seat has its own.

You do not have to keep the email. The key is always under **Licences** at [account.biocognia.com](https://account.biocognia.com/portal/licenses): choose **Show key**, then **Copy**. See [Your licence key](/docs/licensing/licence-key).

## Buying for a lab

Buy as many seats as the lab needs, from your own account. Buying several leaves your account an individual account, with the other seats waiting; inviting the first person makes it a **team**, with the same sign-in, licences and history. You can also make it a team yourself, under **Team**.

The seats you buy, other than your own, wait in the account until you give them out. Invite people under **Team › Members**, then give each a seat under **Team › Seats**. Each person is emailed their own licence key when they are given a seat, and sees it under their own Licences. Owners and administrators see every seat's key on the licence, for the times somebody cannot find theirs.

## After a trial

Buying with the account you started the [trial](/docs/getting-started/free-and-pro#start-a-free-trial) with moves your seat onto the Pro licence. Your computers stay connected and nothing needs to be activated again. The email that confirms it carries your licence key, which is the same key as before.

## More seats later

Under **Billing › Add capacity**, **Another person** adds seats to a licence you hold, charged for what is left of its year, so they end with it. The page shows the amount before you pay; then invite them under **Team › Members** and give each a seat. Adding seats never makes a team by itself: the first invitation does. A licence sold as a subscription changes its seats under **Billing › Purchases** instead.

A second licence, bought the same way as the first, has its own renewal date and can be cancelled on its own. Choose that when the money comes from a different grant.

## Quotes, purchase orders and invoices

An institution that cannot pay by card [requests a quote](https://account.biocognia.com/portal/quotes) from the portal, with the number of seats, the term and a purchase-order number if it has one. Accept the quote and Paddle issues the invoice, payable by bank transfer or card. The licence starts when the invoice is paid. Its seats are then given out from the portal, and each person is emailed their key as they get one.

## Receipts, the card and cancelling

**Billing › Purchases** lists every order, and **Receipts and card** opens Paddle's own page, where invoices can be downloaded and the card on file changed. **Cancel renewal** lets the licence run to the end of the paid term and then stop. When a licence ends, Plexora goes back to Free on every computer, and everything you made stays yours.
