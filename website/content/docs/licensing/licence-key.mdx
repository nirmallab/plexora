Every Plexora Pro seat has a licence key, such as `BIOC-7F3K-9Q2M-XW4T-7KQ2`. It connects a computer to your seat, with no browser needed. The same key works on each of your computers and keeps working when the subscription renews.

## Where to find it

**In your email.** The key is emailed to you when you are given a seat: when you buy one for yourself, when an administrator gives you one, or when a trial seat moves onto a Pro licence (the key is unchanged then). A replaced key is emailed too.

**In your account, at any time.** Sign in at [account.biocognia.com](https://account.biocognia.com/portal/licenses) and open **Licences**. Each seat shows its key masked, as `BIOC-••••-••••-••••-7KQ2`. Choose **Show key**, then **Copy**. Every showing is recorded on the seat. The owners and administrators of a team also see the keys of its seats, for the times somebody cannot find theirs.

## Use it

<Tabs items={['In Plexora', 'Command line', 'HPC cluster']}>
<Tab value="In Plexora">

Open **Settings › License**, choose **Licence key**, paste the key and choose **Activate**. A name is optional; it is how the computer is listed in your account.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Command line">

```bash
plexora license activate BIOC-7F3K-9Q2M-XW4T-7KQ2
```

On a shared machine, keep the key out of your shell history and the process list: pipe it in with `-`, or read it from a file.

```bash
plexora license activate - < ~/plexora-key
plexora license activate --key-file ~/plexora-key
```

`plexora license status` then shows Pro, what the licence includes and when it ends.

</Tab>
<Tab value="HPC cluster">

Run it once on a login node, with `--cluster`:

```bash
plexora license activate BIOC-7F3K-9Q2M-XW4T-7KQ2 --cluster
```

Every node, job and notebook that shares that `$HOME` is then licensed, as one registration on your seat.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## On how many computers

A seat covers up to two computers at a time, unless your licence says otherwise. Each computer you activate takes one place; **Licences** lists them with when each was last seen. To move to a new computer, free a place first: **Sign out** beside the old one in your account, or **Release This Device** in its Settings › License (`plexora license deactivate`).

## If the key leaks

Choose **Replace key** under **Licences**. The old key stops working at once, the computers it already connected stay connected, and the new key is emailed to you. Anyone with your key can take a place on your seat, so do not share it.

When a seat is released or a licence ends, its key stops working.

## Other ways to connect

- **Connect this device**, in Settings › License or `plexora license activate` with no key: Plexora shows a short code, you approve it in the browser, and the licence arrives, for every BioCognia product you hold a seat for.
- **A one-time activation code** (`BIOC-XXXX-XXXX`) or a **`BIOCT1_` token** for automation goes in the same field and the same command.
- **An offline licence file** (`.bioc`), for a computer with no internet access: run `plexora license fingerprint --out fp.json` there, upload the file at account.biocognia.com, and install the `.bioc` file you get back in Settings › License › Offline file, or with `plexora license install`.
